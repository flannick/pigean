# PIGEAN optimization and convergence tracker

Updated 2026-10-13; experiments began 2026-10-12. This is the master record. T = T2D; N = NAFLD. All extensions are **opt-in**, with `legacy` still the default. The published first baseline is [`optimization-milestone-1`](https://github.com/flannick/pigean/tree/optimization-milestone-1); current integration worktree is `worktrees/optimization-stage-2`. Independent fPS/variance branches remain separate; abandoned blockwise/debug designs are excluded.

Use `--gibbs-engine parallel-prefilter` for the first eight cumulative optimizations, then repeat `--gibbs-enable NAME` for additional options or `--gibbs-disable NAME` to remove any preset feature. Each feature remains individually selectable. Missing prerequisites are reported inactive; they are not silently enabled. Defaults and all original presets are preserved. [Feature instructions](../worktrees/optimization-stage-2/docs/experiments/independent_features.md).

Memory follow-up: under stage tracing, cached and compact covariance both reached about 519.4 MiB of tracked allocations, while RSS ordering reversed versus the earlier pairs. Preserve row 4 measurements, but do not attribute its earlier 13–21% RSS increase causally to covariance storage. Lean regression reduced tracked allocations; instrumented timings are not performance estimates.

Known inherited compatibility issue: constant annotations collinear with the intercept can have different SEs under grouped versus legacy arithmetic because of cancellation. The cache reproduces its grouped parent exactly. The trait-screen agreement above concerns priors and coefficients; it does not establish universal SE parity. This remains a limitation; no default/main promotion is claimed.

The full annotation-correlation cache tradeoff is measured in its separate row: it delivered a further 1.100x full T2D speedup at a 184 MiB retained-memory cost. Fusing the remaining grouped moment/contraction work offers smaller incremental gains; correlation preparation is already compiled and weights change with each response. That remaining fusion is an unimplemented engineering option, not a measured speedup. No further order-of-magnitude arithmetic gain is suggested by the final profile.
