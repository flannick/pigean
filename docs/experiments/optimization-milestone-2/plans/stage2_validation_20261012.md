1. Finish the running 500-update T2D retained arithmetic combination and its separate 101-update profile. Compare against both completed 500-update controls: published milestone and the two-feature inner implementation. Record priors, uncorrected/corrected betas, memory, inner time and diagnostics. Treat historical legacy timing as cross-session evidence.
2. Run one serial 500-update NAFLD milestone/retained pair, each capped at 30 minutes. This extends the arithmetic check to the second trait without multiplying seeds or repeating the long T2D legacy fit. Do not infer posterior convergence from completion.
3. On T2D, run a 101-update reference followed by earlier-check and conditional-mean probes, individually. Keep the same arithmetic features in each. Reject timeouts, scale shifts or insufficient gains; extend a promising inference probe to 500 updates only after inspecting the short result. Do not combine two unvalidated inference changes. For screening, a useful candidate should save at least roughly 10% total inference time while keeping priors/uncorrected-beta Pearson above 0.98 and slopes within 0.9–1.1; these are pragmatic investigation thresholds, not validation of its posterior or uncertainty. A visibly shifted scale or a negligible gain is grounds to stop after the small screen.
4. Before the inference probes, evaluate the remaining static-correlation reconstruction cost with one bounded content-validated cache experiment. Its 256 MiB cap, cold setup, exact matrix agreement and full memory cost decide whether to keep it separate or add it. Preserve the validated six-feature result as a frozen comparison. Use the final profile to screen remaining arithmetic ideas. Prioritize a step only if its measured share is material (roughly 10% or more) and a concrete change could save appreciable total time. Document remaining costs and limits instead of chasing noise or tiny gains. No cache of changing sampled responses/covariance values.
5. Archive compact reproducible commands, fixtures' hashes, source commits, test reports, full-run comparisons and decisions with the retained source. Commit each implementation independently and keep legacy default. Push tested checkpoints, verify remote refs, and tag a second arithmetic milestone if both trait checks support it. Keep rejected residual and inference experiments visibly labelled.
6. End with all launched jobs accounted for and a tracker containing measured gains, memory, agreement, flags, default status, scaling expectations and retention decisions. Keep convergence research separate from arithmetic validation.

The direct Gaussian-VI substitution, persistent joint chain, collapsed/support-MH methods and tempering require a specified compatible target. The captured inner-conditionals audit supplies a concrete reason they are not overnight drop-in fixes. No claimed convergence improvement without appropriate evidence.
