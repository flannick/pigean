# Next inference work after the arithmetic pass

The next substantial gain should target the number of steps needed for reliable estimates or replace the repeated inference procedure. In the latest full T2D run, inner fits occupy 41.85% of 309.42 seconds: even eliminating all remaining inner work would provide only a further 1.72x at unchanged outer workload. The two simple inference shortcuts did not provide sufficient gains or a qualitative convergence change.

1. Define the scientific estimands and a compatible target. Decide how marginal logistic estimates, heterogeneous standard errors, annotation correlations, prior-dependent HuGE refresh and averaged-coefficient feedback enter the model. The [inner-target audit](../logs/2026-10-12_inner_target_audit.md) shows why a textbook Gaussian VI objective is not a justified drop-in replacement for the current conditional equations. Preserve a model-version flag for any deliberate target change.
2. Build reduced and simulated reference problems with interchangeable correlated annotations, heterogeneous standard errors and known gene-level signal. Use trusted integration or exhaustive support calculations where tractable. Validate uncertainty, scale and mode coverage, not just agreement with a non-converged legacy run.
3. Compare one approximation and one mode-exploration method on that defined target. Structured/mixture variational inference is a speed candidate; valid collapsed support moves or tempering are mixing candidates. Logistic spike-and-slab approximations have precedent in [Carbonetto and Stephens](https://pcarbo.github.io/bvs.pdf). [Polson, Scott and Windle](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.0310) provide logistic data augmentation; [Jankowiak](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.01180) develops scalable variable-selection MCMC for generalized linear models. These papers motivate methods, not PIGEAN speed or calibration claims.
4. Advance only a validated reduced-problem method to matched T2D/NAFLD tests. Measure total cost, calibration and uncertainty alongside priors, uncorrected/corrected betas, mode movement and MCMC diagnostics where applicable. Maintain independent flags and the tagged arithmetic/legacy controls. Keep trait configurations explicit and avoid a large parameter grid.
